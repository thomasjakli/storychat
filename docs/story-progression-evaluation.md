# Story progression: bounded live evaluation

2026-10-31. Six explicitly authorized subscription requests, three original synthetic paired situations, same selected model (`gpt-6.1-sol`), no failures or retries. No private character research, real conversations or production data. Full synthetic results remain in ignored analysis storage; this document records a public-safe independent review.

## Method and limitations

Compared old provider and context guidance with new guidance on identical narrative inputs and source IDs. Both variants used the current reserved-reply source contract. This is a prompt-guidance comparison, not a replay of the complete historical application, an evaluation of the enlarged recent-context window, or evidence of long-term memory quality. One sample per condition/scenario, unblinded human-style review by an independent agent. No statistical claim or claim that repetition is eliminated.

## Observations

| Situation | Before | After | Independent assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet observatory visit | Offers quiet company and lets visitor choose an activity | Introduces a practical ceiling drip and map work as optional points of interest | More concrete initiative and discoverable details; does not decide visitor response |
| Repeated reassurance in a greenhouse | Stops repeating the exact reassurance, offers tour or wandering | Develops an unfinished sign, dry humor and donated plants | More material to explore; baseline already displayed some initiative |
| German watchmaker scene | Describes a troublesome watch and invites closer inspection | Demonstrates an orientation-dependent fault and offers a magnifier | Clear on-screen development, preserved German and player choice; baseline was not entirely static |

All three after variants produced state proposals supported by their visible reply and cited the reserved assistant reply source. All submitted source IDs were allowed. Before variants returned empty state updates. No obvious fabricated shared history, forced player decisions, automatic intimacy or contradictory continuity appeared in these short samples. The scenarios were too short to establish sustained relationship pacing or resistance to repetition over many turns.

After replies were longer in every pair: 1,047 versus370, 1,056 versus278, and979 versus620 characters. More description is part of the observed difference; it must not be equated automatically with better storytelling. Owner acceptance should assess relevance, desired response length and pacing in longer stories.
